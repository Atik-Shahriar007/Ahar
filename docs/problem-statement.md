ভালো থাকুন, নিজের মতো। — Be well, in your own way.

THE PROBLEM
Knowing that you should eat better does not tell you what to order at a Tejgaon lunch stall, what to cook after class, or how to stretch this week's bazar money.

Bangladesh needs food guidance that fits people's actual meals, purchasing power and working lives. A university student with irregular meals and a rickshaw driver doing strenuous work should not receive the same calorie-cutting advice. Neither should need imported ingredients, a gym membership, or fluency in nutrition terminology to begin.

AharAI turns a familiar meal into a manageable next step. It combines a person's preferences, routine, food history and budget with local food information to suggest what to keep, what to adjust and what to buy next. Family planning is an optional extension of the same personal service.

EVIDENCE, WITH THE DATE LEFT ON
Bangladesh's 2018 national STEPS survey documents dietary risk factors. Use the original report and its stated population when citing a statistic; do not relabel historical results as 2026 prevalence. FAO's Bangladesh dietary guidance recommends varied, balanced meals using familiar food groups. See sources.md for direct primary sources and competition evidence.

WHAT WE MUST VALIDATE
Our hypothesis is that existing guidance often feels detached from local dishes, budgets and routines. We have not conducted user research or proven that no competitor offers these features. Interview people who eat at home and outside across different budgets before making that claim in a submission.

THE PERSON WE HELP
Anyone who wants everyday food decisions to feel easier. Our first pilot would involve consenting adults, including students, workers and household meal planners. A smartphone demo does not establish access for everyone: Bangla input, short voice interactions and a lighter interface need separate field testing.

THE MOST IMPORTANT DISTINCTION
Health is not defined by visible abs, thinness, class, or a comparison with Europeans. Food choices are influenced by money, time, access, preference, work and health needs. AharAI responds to those conditions without shaming a body or a traditional meal.

ONE-SENTENCE SUBMISSION VERSION
AharAI helps people in Bangladesh turn familiar meals and real budgets into practical food improvements, using AI to personalize advice to their daily routine and learn from the choices they can actually sustain.
